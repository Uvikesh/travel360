**Channel / brand: The StorySpark** ("हर कहानी में छिपी है एक चिंगारी…"). Logo: `assets/brand/storyspark_logo.png`.
Put it on every video (top-left corner + a big logo on the end card) and on every thumbnail. Do not use a text watermark.

Free tier = 10 TTS requests/day, so make one request per episode (whole script). The API key comes from

is stripped automatically, but always verify by transcribing the narration (gemini-2.5-flash) before the final render.

## Goal: every video goes to YouTube Shorts FOR MONETIZATION (YouTube Partner Program)
The owner uploads these Shorts to earn from Shorts ad revenue. Every decision must keep the video monetizable:

1. **Copyright-safe assets only (commercial use allowed).**
   - Images: own / AI-generated by the owner / CC0 / Public Domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA, with attribution kept in
     `output/<episode>/credits.txt` and pasted into the YouTube description. Never use NC (non-commercial) or ND
     licences, Google Images, or screenshots from TV, films or other creators.
   - Music: the synthesized bed from `engine/audio.py`, YouTube Audio Library tracks, or properly licensed tracks.
     No Bollywood or trending songs inside the rendered file (add trending sounds in the YouTube app instead, if wanted).
   - Map data: CC BY 4.0 (svg-maps) + Natural Earth. Credit them in the description.
2. **Avoid "inauthentic / mass-produced / reused content"** (YPP policy). Each video needs real value:
   an original script, new facts, a unique angle, our own edit. Do not copy another channel's script,
   and do not upload near-duplicate templates in bulk. The owner's own voice-over is the strongest signal of
   originality. AI TTS is allowed, but the script and edit must clearly be ours.
3. **AI disclosure:** use YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" toggle when realistic AI people or voices are
   used, and say "AI-generated illustrations" in the description for AI portraits.
4. **Facts must be correct.** No misinformation (it hurts ad suitability). Keep it advertiser-friendly: no
   violence or graphic images, no hate, no caste/religion/region put-downs, no politics, no swearing.
5. **Map of India must follow the official boundary** (full J&K + Ladakh). A wrong map can get the video
   reported or blocked in India.
6. **Shorts format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, ≤ 3 min (aim for 60–90 s), a strong hook in the first 2 s, an end frame
   that loops, and a CTA that brings comments.
7. Voice/script language: Hinglish (Devanagari), conversational, energetic. Not robotic, not a plain list:
   every ~10 s needs a surprise fact, a question or a twist.

## Always use a Hindi font (owner's standing rule)
- **All on-screen text is written in Hindi (Devanagari script) and set in the Hindi font**
  `assets/fonts/NotoSansDevanagari-*.ttf`. That covers name tags, titles, thumbnail text, CTA pills and the watermark.
  Write English words in Devanagari (स्टेट, डिश, जी आई टैग), and write numbers as digits inside Hindi text.
- Do not use Latin/Poppins text on screen (Poppins exists only as a technical fallback).
- **Talk to the owner in Hindi written in Devanagari** in chat (not Roman Hinglish). Technical names such as
  files, commands and code may stay in English.
