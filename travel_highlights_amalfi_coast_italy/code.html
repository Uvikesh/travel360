About the Amalfi Coast

Suspended between the sky and the iridescent sea, the Amalfi Coast seems born from the palette of a romantic painter. This 50-kilometer stretch of coastline in the Campania region is renowned globally for its dramatic topography, picturesque towns, and historical significance, earning it a spot as a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Its history is as rich as its landscapes. Once the heart of the powerful Maritime Republic of Amalfi in the 10th and 11th centuries, the region was a bustling hub of trade between the Byzantine Empire and the western world. Today, the remnants of this glorious past are woven into the narrow alleys, majestic cathedrals, and terraced lemon groves that cling to the cliffs.

Visitors are captivated by the vibrant hues of Positano, the serene elegance of Ravello, and the historical charm of Amalfi itself. Whether you are navigating the winding coastal roads, savoring fresh seafood overlooking the Mediterranean, or hiking ancient trails, the Amalfi Coast offers an immersive experience of 'la dolce vita'.
